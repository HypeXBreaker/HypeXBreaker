### ☕ About Me

I am a student deeply interested in **AI/ML, Cybersecurity, and Networking**. My focus is on building robust tools, websites, agents, APIs, and SaaS platforms. I leverage automated custom tools and MCPs to streamline workflows.

Currently, I am focused on being a **Full Stack Developer**, actively participating in hackathons, with a long-term goal of shifting towards **AI Model Development** for specialized applications.

Many assume I just "vibe code," but they don't see the rigorous process and learning behind it. While others might rush to build and sell sites—often resulting in heavy errors, vulnerabilities, and easy targets for brute forcing—I take a different approach. I believe in understanding the core architecture. I always **plan, learn, build, find errors, and perfect** my projects until the very end, ensuring they are secure and resilient.

> *"A simple structure, robust code, and powerful capabilities can elevate any project to production level."*
